Featured Case

Mobile Cinema — Reaching Remote Communities Through Film

Challenge

Reaching remote mountain communities in Timor-Leste where traditional media doesn't reach — bringing health awareness and community stories to thousands of people.

Production

Custom mobile cinema setup with generator, projection screen and surround sound — deployed across 40+ villages over 6 months with local facilitators.

Result

30,000+ community members reached. Measurable increase in health-seeking behaviour in target communities. Model replicated by 3 partner organizations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What production services does Pixelasia offer in Timor-Leste?

Pixelasia offers full-service video production including documentaries, commercials, social behaviour change campaigns, event coverage, radio production, and production support for international crews filming in Timor-Leste. The company has produced over 100 projects since 2011.

Can Pixelasia support international film crews in Dili?

Yes. Pixelasia provides local production services for international crews and agencies, including location scouting, permits, local crew hiring, equipment, and logistics across Timor-Leste. The team includes crew members from 6 countries.

What is the Dili International Film Festival (DIFF)?

DIFF is an annual film festival organized by Pixelasia since 2019 in Dili, Timor-Leste. It features screenings, masterclasses, and cross-cultural exchange, connecting local filmmakers with international voices and building Southeast Asia's newest film community.

About Us

"We believe stories from Timor-Leste deserve to be told with the same craft and care as anywhere in the world — because they do."

"From mountain villages to international festivals, we bring Timorese stories to audiences near and far."

"Production is a craft, but impact is the mission. We measure success in communities reached and lives changed."

Founded in 2011 by Lena Lenzen, Pixelasia Productions Dili is the only full-service production house in Timor-Leste. With a crew from 6 countries and over 100 productions under our belt, we partner with UN agencies, governments, NGOs and independent clients to create content that moves people — literally and figuratively. We're based in Dili and we work across the entire country.
